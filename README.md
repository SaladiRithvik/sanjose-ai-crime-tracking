# SJSU SafeMap: AI for tracking crime in San Jose

Client: City of San José, UPD
Team: Rithvik Saladi, Eric Liao, James Doan, Taran Sahota, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I worked on the record system and transcribing incidents.

## The problem
San Jose residents and UPD crime analysts lack a clear, timely way to turn scattered incident reports into location-based patterns. This makes it difficult to identify where safety incidents are clustering around San Jose.

## What we built
- SJSU Safemap turns incidents into a heat map
- Users can submit reports and view the heat map
- UPD analysts will review AI-transcribed reports before approving them for the heat map

## What I would do next
There is a lack of accessibility for non-English speakers, as well as those who are either technologically illiterate or have disabilities. I would integrate some type of call reporting system or provide some type of active translation to accommodate the gap.
