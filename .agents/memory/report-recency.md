---
name: Report recency
description: Meaning and lifetime of the demonstration Recent indicator.
---

The “Recent” tag and report highlight apply exclusively to submissions less than one hour old and disappear when that hour ends, including on an already-open page.

**Why:** The user requested a temporary demonstration indicator on 2026-10-06, not a permanent status or an incident-date label.

**How to apply:** Use actual submission time. Do not reset the hour on approval, refresh, or sign-in, and do not mark old sample reports as recent merely to populate the demonstration.
